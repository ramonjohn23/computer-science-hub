04 | UNDERSTANDING PACKETS

What is a packet?

A packet is a small unit of data transmitted across a network.

When a large message or file is sent, it is divided into smaller units called packets.

04 | WHY SPLIT DATA?

Why is data divided into packets?

Efficient transmission

Small packets can share network links with packets from other users.

Alternative routes

Packets can travel through different available routes.

Error handling

If a packet is lost or corrupted, it may be retransmitted without sending the entire file again.

Network sharing

Multiple devices can use the network at the same time.

07 | PACKET SWITCHING

What is packet switching?

Packet switching is a method of transmitting data in which a message is divided into packets. These packets may travel independently through the network and are reassembled at the destination.

07 | ADVANTAGES

Advantages of packet switching

Efficient use of network

Network links can be shared by packets from many users.

Flexible routing

Packets can be forwarded using alternative routes when a route is unavailable.

Reliable recovery

Lost or corrupted packets can be retransmitted where the protocol supports it.

No dedicated connection

Network capacity is not reserved for one communication for the entire transmission.

07 | DISADVANTAGES

Disadvantages of packet switching

Packets may be delayed

Packets can experience different delays during transmission.

Packets may arrive out of order

The destination needs to arrange them correctly.

Packets may be lost

Some packets may need to be retransmitted.

Network congestion

Heavy traffic can cause delays and packet loss.
